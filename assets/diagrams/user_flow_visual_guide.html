🛡️ Bank-Grade Security

The AI Agent never touches or sees user MPIN, OTP, or account credentials. All authentication is 100% bank-owned within RBI-regulated boundaries.

⚡ Zero-Dropoff Conversion

Eliminates the traditional 3–4 app switching friction. Replaces 18–24 clicks with 2 natural voice prompts, cutting completion time by 83%.

Plug-and-play architecture supporting Zomato, MakeMyTrip, RedBus, and Blinkit with dynamic permission toggles controlled by the user.
